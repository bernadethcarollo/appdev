## My Favorite Movie: Shes dating the gangster

**Genre:** Romance, Drama
**Director:** Cathy Garcia-Molina

### Summary of the Movie
She’s Dating the Gangster is a Filipino romantic movie about a girl named Athena who pretends to be the girlfriend of a bad boy named Kenji to make his ex-girlfriend jealous. At first, they always fight and don’t like each other. But as time goes by, they start to fall in love for real. Their love story faces many problems, especially when Kenji’s first love comes back. The movie also shows another love story connected to them in the present time. In the end, it teaches about true love, forgiveness, and destiny.
